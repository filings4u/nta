01 • Brand foundation

A premium transportation identity with global reach.

The NTA Logistics identity combines deep navy, metallic gold, electric blue, silver, a connected globe, and a roadway motif. Together, these elements communicate movement, reach, reliability, coordination, and premium service.

The master logo carries rich metallic effects and detail. Supporting brand materials should stay cleaner and more restrained so the logo remains the focal point.

02 • Logo system

The master mark is the primary brand signature.

Use the supplied artwork rather than recreating the NTA monogram with live type. The letterforms and road integration are custom visual elements.

Preferred • Light background
Preferred • Dark background
Clear space

Keep open space around the logo equal to at least the height of the lower tagline band.

Minimum size

Use the full detailed logo at sizes where the globe, roadway, and tagline remain legible.

Do not distort

Never stretch, skew, recolor, crop, or separate pieces of the master artwork.

Large-format work

For vehicle wraps and oversized signage, use a true vector redraw when available.

03 • Color system

Deep blue leads. Gold signals premium service.

The logo contains gradients and metallic effects. These flat colors are the practical working palette for web, print, presentations, documents, and campaigns.

Strong display type. Clean operational copy.

06 • Brand voice

Direct, capable, responsive, and business-focused.

NTA should sound like an experienced transportation partner: confident without exaggeration, clear about what it does, and focused on solving commercial freight needs.

Say

“Dependable transportation coordination.”

Clear, credible language grounded in the service being delivered.

Say

“Connecting qualified capacity with commercial freight.”

Emphasizes NTA’s brokerage and coordination role accurately.

Avoid

Overpromising or generic hype.

Prefer specific operational value over unsupported claims like “the best logistics company.”
