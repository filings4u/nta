Cookie Policy

1. About Cookies

Cookies are small data files stored by a browser. Websites may also use local storage, session storage, pixels, server logs, and similar technologies. This policy explains how NTA Logistics LLC may use these technologies on ntalogistics.com.

2. Technologies We May Use

  • Essential technologies used to operate forms, secure sessions, route requests, preserve workflow state, and protect against abuse.
  • Preference technologies that remember choices or temporary form state.
  • Performance technologies that may help us understand website performance, errors, traffic sources, and feature usage.
  • Third-party content technologies that may be used when pages load fonts, maps, videos, or other embedded resources from another provider.

3. Why We Use These Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technology to keep the website functional, maintain application state, support authentication, reduce fraudulent or abusive submissions, troubleshoot errors, understand website performance, and improve user experience.

4. Third-Party Technology

Some website features may be delivered by third-party service providers. Those providers may process technical information under their own privacy terms. The presence and behavior of third-party technologies may change as website features are added or removed.

5. Your Controls

Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies and site data. Restricting essential browser storage may cause forms, logins, or other website functions to stop working correctly. Browser settings vary by provider and device.

6. Local & Session Storage

NTA workflows may use browser local or session storage to retain short-lived application references, workflow state, or other information needed to complete a user-initiated process. Clearing browser site data may remove that information.

7. Policy Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy when website technology or practices change.

8. Contact

Questions about website tracking or browser storage may be sent to support@ntalogistics.com or by calling 803-629-0227.
