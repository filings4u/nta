About NTA Logistics

A freight brokerage built around dependable coordination.

NTA Logistics LLC connects qualified transportation carriers with businesses that need reliable freight, hauling, and commercial transportation solutions.

Our Role

We bring transportation requirements and qualified capacity together.

NTA Logistics serves as the coordination point between shippers, contractors, suppliers, developers, and transportation carriers. Our job is to understand the freight requirement, communicate it clearly, and help connect the right transportation capacity to the opportunity.

We are building the company around organized intake, professional carrier onboarding, responsive communication, and scalable technology—so clients and carriers can work with NTA efficiently as the network grows.
