Before You Begin

One application establishes your shipper profile.

NTA uses this information to identify your company, establish billing contacts, understand expected freight activity, and evaluate requested credit terms.

Have this information ready

  • Legal company and tax information
  • Accounts payable and invoicing contacts
  • Requested credit amount and payment terms
  • Typical freight volume and equipment needs
  • Three business / trade references
  • Authorized signer information

By submitting this application, the applicant certifies that the information provided is true and complete to the best of its knowledge and that the signer is authorized to submit this application on behalf of the company.
