How It Works

From freight request to capacity coordination.

  1. Submit the requirement.Tell us what is moving, when, where, and what equipment is required.
  2. NTA reviews the details.We evaluate timing, route, freight characteristics, accessorials, and project constraints.
  3. Capacity is sourced.Qualified carrier options are matched to the transportation requirement.
  4. Communication stays active.NTA coordinates the transportation process with both sides.
