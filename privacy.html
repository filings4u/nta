Privacy Policy

How NTA Logistics handles information submitted through our website, freight workflows, applications, and business communications.

Important: This policy describes NTA Logistics LLC’s website, application, CRM, and online onboarding practices. Separate transportation agreements, rate confirmations, and other contracts may contain additional confidentiality or data provisions.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how NTA Logistics LLC (“NTA,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information through ntalogistics.com and related online forms, portals, applications, and communications. NTA Logistics LLC is a freight brokerage and transportation coordination business located at 200 Haverford Dr, Columbia, South Carolina 29203.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact and company information, such as names, titles, email addresses, telephone numbers, business addresses, and company names.
  • Shipper information, including billing contacts, credit-application information, commodities, service regions, shipping volumes, and invoicing preferences.
  • Quote and shipment information, including origin, destination, dates, equipment needs, commodity descriptions, weights, dimensions, accessorial requests, cargo values, and special instructions.
  • Carrier information, including USDOT/MC information, authority status, equipment, service areas, insurance details, tax information, dispatch and accounting contacts, certifications, and uploaded compliance documents.
  • Carrier payment information, including remittance preferences and, when voluntarily provided for ACH setup, bank and routing information.
  • Freight agent application information, including experience, freight specialties, territories, customer-development background, and work-readiness information.
  • Communications, including messages submitted through contact forms and correspondence related to services or applications.
  • Technical and source data, such as browser/device information, IP-derived security signals, referring pages, campaign parameters, and similar data used for security and website operations.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to respond to inquiries; prepare and evaluate freight quotes; establish shipper records; qualify and onboard carriers; review freight agent applications; coordinate transportation services; process remittance instructions; communicate about submissions; send transactional confirmations; maintain CRM records; prevent fraud and abuse; secure our systems; comply with legal obligations; and manage our business operations.

4. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information to service providers that support hosting, database services, email delivery, storage, authentication, payment or banking workflows, analytics, security, and business operations; to shippers, carriers, facilities, insurers, claims professionals, or other parties when reasonably necessary to coordinate transportation or resolve a business matter; and to government authorities, regulators, courts, or other parties when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, property, or the integrity of our services.

We do not authorize service providers to use information for purposes unrelated to providing services to NTA, subject to their own legal obligations and contractual terms.

5. Financial, Banking & Sensitive Carrier Data

NTA may collect payment instructions when a carrier elects to provide them online. Sensitive banking values are handled separately from the ordinary carrier application record and are intended to be protected using restricted-access server-side processing and encrypted storage. Carrier documents are stored in restricted storage and are not intended to be exposed through unrestricted public links.

Do not send payment credentials, passwords, or other sensitive information through ordinary email unless NTA specifically requests an approved secure method.

6. Cookies & Online Data

Our website may use browser storage, session storage, cookies, or similar technologies that are necessary to operate forms, preserve workflow state, secure sessions, remember preferences, and measure website performance. See our Cookie Policy for more information.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including customer and carrier administration, compliance, accounting, claims, dispute resolution, security, recordkeeping, and legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by record type and business relationship.

8. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. No website, network, storage system, or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their own devices, accounts, and email systems.

9. Your Choices & Privacy Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate business or legal retention requirements. Depending on where you reside, additional privacy rights may apply. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

To make a privacy request, contact NTA using the information below and describe the request and the information involved.

10. Children’s Privacy

The website and services are intended for commercial and business use and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through our commercial onboarding and freight-service forms.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, technology, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of the policy will identify the current version.
