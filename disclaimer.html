1. Freight Brokerage Status

NTA Logistics LLC is a transportation property broker operating under USDOT 4565802 and MC 1815428. NTA arranges transportation with independent motor carriers and does not represent itself as the motor carrier transporting freight unless a separate written arrangement expressly states otherwise.

2. General Website Information

Website content is provided for general business and informational purposes. Descriptions of services, equipment, industries, processes, or transportation practices are not guarantees that a particular shipment can be handled in a particular manner.

3. No Guarantee of Capacity, Rates, Timing, or Results

Submitting a quote request or discussing a shipment does not guarantee carrier availability, rate, transit time, pickup, delivery, or other result. Shipment commitments are subject to the applicable written quote, Rate Confirmation, agreement, carrier acceptance, and operating conditions.

4. Independent Motor Carriers

Motor carriers engaged through NTA are independent transportation providers responsible for their own drivers, equipment, authority, safety compliance, and performance as provided by law and contract. Qualification procedures are designed to support broker operations but cannot eliminate every transportation risk.

5. Cargo Claims, Insurance & Loss

Statements about insurance, cargo claims, carrier qualification, or risk management on the website are general information only. Actual coverage depends on policy terms, exclusions, limits, endorsements, applicable law, and the facts of a loss. Claims should be reported promptly through the appropriate NTA contact and handled under the applicable agreements and legal requirements.

6. No Legal, Tax, Insurance, or Regulatory Advice

Website articles, guides, forms, and explanations do not constitute legal, tax, insurance, safety, accounting, or regulatory advice. Businesses should consult appropriate professionals regarding obligations that apply to their specific operations.
