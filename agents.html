Who Should Apply

Experienced freight professionals ready to grow.

This application is designed for freight brokers, freight agents, transportation sales professionals, and experienced logistics professionals who can develop and manage commercial freight relationships.

Helpful background

  • Freight brokerage, logistics, dispatch, or transportation sales experience
  • Established or developing shipper relationships
  • Knowledge of rates, lanes, equipment, and carrier sourcing
  • Strong customer communication and follow-through
  • Comfort with TMS, CRM, email, and remote business tools
  • Understanding of ethical brokerage and no-double-brokering standards
